Non-Repudiation

Discover how cybersecurity ensures accountability. Learn how systems create undeniable proof of identity and actions, guaranteeing that a user cannot falsely deny their digital footprint.

Non-Repudiation Fundamentals

  • The Core Concept: Ensuring a user cannot deny having performed a specific action or transaction.
  • Building Trust: Crucial for accountability, legal protection, and preventing fraud or disputes.
  • Digital Receipts: Functions as irrefutable evidence of an event, such as a signed document or a sent email.

The AAA Triad

  • Authentication: Proving the identity of the user (e.g., passwords, biometrics, MFA).
  • Authorization: Defining and enforcing what the authenticated user is allowed to do.
  • Accounting: Tracking and logging exactly what the user did and when they did it.

Digital Evidence & Logs

  • Timestamps & IPs: Crucial metadata that places an action at a specific time and physical location.
  • Digital Signatures: Cryptographic math that binds a user's identity to a specific file or message.
  • Log Integrity: Ensuring logs cannot be tampered with, often using hashing and WORM storage.
