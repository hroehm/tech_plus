Security Awareness

Understanding phishing and other vectors for malicious content is critical to protecting yourself and others from social engineering attacks[cite: 4].

Social Engineering & Phishing

  • Social Engineering: Manipulating people into revealing confidential information or performing insecure actions[cite: 2, 4].
  • Phishing: Using fraudulent emails or messages to trick victims into giving up passwords or downloading malware[cite: 2, 4].
  • Targeted Attacks: Spear phishing targets specific individuals, while whaling specifically targets high-profile individuals[cite: 2, 4].

Web Vectors & Malicious Content

  • Attack Vector: The method or pathway a malicious actor uses to gain unauthorized access[cite: 4].
  • Typosquatting: Using misspelled domains that look legitimate to trick users into visiting malicious sites[cite: 2, 4].
  • Watering Hole Attacks: Infecting a commonly visited, weaker website to gain access to a more secure target[cite: 2, 4].
