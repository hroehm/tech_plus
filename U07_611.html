Confidentiality Concerns & Threats

Data security requires continuous vigilance. Explore the CIA Triad and the critical threats aimed at compromising the confidentiality of digital and physical information.

The CIA Triad & Confidentiality

  • CIA Triad: The foundational model of cybersecurity: Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability.
  • Confidentiality: Ensuring data is only viewable and accessible by authorized individuals.
  • Sensitive Data: Passwords, PII (Names, SSNs), and financial/health records are prime targets.

Network Sniffing

  • Sniffing/Snooping: Monitoring and capturing network packets as they move through a system.
  • Vulnerabilities: Unencrypted traffic over public Wi-Fi can expose login credentials instantly.
  • Defense: Secure protocols like HTTPS, WPA3 for wireless, and VPNs encrypt the traffic.

Social Engineering

  • Psychological Tactics: Manipulating human behavior, fear, or urgency to gain information.
  • Phishing: Using fake emails (Phishing), texts (Smishing), or voice calls (Vishing) to steal data.
  • Mitigation: User training, ignoring suspicious requests, and reporting through official channels.

Physical & Communication Threats

  • In-Person Observation: Shoulder surfing (watching screens) and Tailgating (following through doors).
  • Monitoring: Eavesdropping (listening) and Wiretapping (intercepting physical/wireless calls).
  • Dumpster Diving: Stealing discarded physical documents; mitigated by shredding.
