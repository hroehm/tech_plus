Encryption & Data Types

Understanding how encryption protects data based on its current state: Data at Rest and Data in Transit.

Data at Rest: File & Disk

  • Data at Rest: Digital information stored physically that is not actively moving across networks.
  • File-Level Encryption: Protects specific, individual files or folders on a device.
  • Disk-Level Encryption: Protects the entire storage volume (Full Disk Encryption), securing everything if the hardware is lost.

Data at Rest: Mobile Devices

  • Mobile Encryption: Specialized protection for highly portable, easily lost devices like smartphones and tablets.
  • Built-In Mechanisms: Modern mobile OSs encrypt storage by default out of the box.
  • Authentication: Relies on strong PINs, passwords, or biometrics to unlock the decryption key.

Data in Transit: Email & HTTPS

  • Data in Transit: Data actively moving from one location to another across a network.
  • Email Encryption: Uses TLS (server-to-server) or End-to-End Encryption (E2EE) to stop interception.
  • HTTPS: Secures website traffic (like passwords/credit cards) using SSL/TLS, indicated by a padlock.

Data in Transit: VPN & Apps

  • VPN (Virtual Private Network): Creates a secure, encrypted tunnel masking the user's IP and securing all traffic.
  • Mobile App Encryption: Uses transport encryption (TLS) and application-level encryption for data moving from apps to servers.
  • Public Wi-Fi Defense: Essential tools for protecting data on open, untrusted networks.
