AAA Authorization

Explore how systems determine exactly what you are allowed to do after you log in, ensuring data confidentiality and system integrity through robust Access Control.

AAA Triad & Authorization

  • Authentication: Proving who you are (e.g., entering a password).
  • Authorization: Determining what actions you are permitted to take.
  • Accounting: Tracking and logging user actions for accountability.

Access Control Methods

  • MAC (Mandatory): Strict control by a central authority or system.
  • DAC (Discretionary): The data owner decides who gets access.
  • RBAC (Role-Based): Access depends on your job role/title.
  • Rule-Based: Access depends on system rules (time, location).

Permission Types

  • Read: Allows viewing the contents of a file or folder.
  • Write: Allows modifying or saving changes to a file.
  • Execute: Allows running a program, script, or application.
  • Delete: Allows completely removing a file or resource.

Accounts & Least Privilege

  • Administrator: Elevated privileges with full system control.
  • User Accounts: Limited access restricted to personal tasks/data.
  • Least Privilege Model: Granting users *only* the minimum access needed to do their jobs.
