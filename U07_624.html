Safe Browsing Practices

Learn how to tell if a website is safe to use and why it is important to check for a valid certificate when browsing online to protect your sensitive data[cite: 154].

Safe Browsing Fundamentals

  • HTTPS & Links: Use secure websites and be cautious with unfamiliar links and attachments[cite: 4].
  • Passwords & MFA: Utilize strong, unique passwords and enable Multi-Factor Authentication[cite: 173, 176].
  • Networks & Updates: Avoid public Wi-Fi without VPNs and always keep your browser software updated[cite: 182, 184].

Web Certificates

  • Digital Identity: Certificates establish secure connections and verify website identities[cite: 6].
  • Certificate Authorities: Trusted organizations (CAs) that issue and validate these digital certificates[cite: 8].
  • Valid vs. Invalid: A valid cert is issued by a trusted CA, matches the domain, and is not expired or revoked[cite: 13].
