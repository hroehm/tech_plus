Internet of Things (IoT) Devices & Communication

Smart devices are transforming our homes, workplaces, and daily routines. Explore the core communication protocols, appliances, automation systems, vehicles, media devices, medical monitors, exercise gear, and wearables that comprise the IoT ecosystem.

IoT Communication

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, NFC, Cellular, LoRaWAN, Ethernet, and Powerline Communication protocols linking smart devices.

Home Appliances

Smart refrigerators, washers, and pet feeders featuring remote control, automation, and energy monitoring.

Home Automation

Thermostats, security systems, smart assistants, smart locks, and video doorbells for complete residence integration.

IoT Vehicles

Connected automobiles utilizing telematics, real-time GPS route optimization, and remote diagnostics.

IP / Security Cameras

Internet-connected surveillance cameras providing high-definition live feeds and motion detection alerts.

Streaming Media

Smart TVs and streaming boxes delivering on-demand internet video, voice control, and recommendations.

Medical & Fitness

Remote patient monitors, smart scales, and connected workout bikes tracking health metrics and metrics.

Wearable Devices

Smartwatches and fitness bands providing continuous health tracking, sleep monitoring, and notifications.
