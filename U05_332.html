Section 3.3.2

Collaboration Software

Explore the digital tools designed to help individuals and teams work together effectively, communicate instantly, and share resources regardless of physical location.

Communication Tools

  • Email Clients: Asynchronous, formal messaging (e.g., Outlook, Gmail).
  • Instant Messaging: Real-time, quick chats (e.g., Teams, Slack).
  • Management: Contact organization and scheduling integration.

Virtual Meetings & Support

  • Conferencing: Video/audio meetings (e.g., Zoom, Meet).
  • Remote Support: IT troubleshooting via remote access.
  • Features: Screen sharing, session recording, and secure connections.

Collaborative Creation

  • Online Workspaces: Central hubs for projects and tasks.
  • Document Sharing: Simultaneous real-time editing.
  • Control: Version tracking, link sharing, and access permissions.

Information Access

  • Web Browsers: Applications to navigate the internet.
  • Organization: Bookmarks, tabs, and history management.
  • Enhancement: Extensions, search engines, and privacy controls.
