Password Best Practices

Passwords are the first line of defense in the digital world. They act as digital keys protecting sensitive data, identity, and assets. Learn the critical rules to ensure your digital keys are unbreakable.

Length & Complexity

  • Length is King: Passwords must be at least 12 characters; 16+ is highly recommended.
  • Complexity: Must include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters (!, @, #).
  • Passphrases: Using multiple words (e.g., Red$Monkey@RunsFast) maximizes length and memorability.

History & Expiration

  • Password History: Policies that prevent users from reusing their previous passwords.
  • Password Expiration: Mandates changing passwords after a set period (e.g., 30, 60, 90 days).
  • Risk Reduction: Reduces the time a stolen, compromised password remains valid on a network.

Privacy & Site Reuse

  • No Sharing: Passwords should NEVER be shared with friends, family, or IT support.
  • Avoid Reuse: Never use the same password across multiple websites.
  • Credential Stuffing: Attackers use breached passwords from one site to unlock your other accounts.

Managers, Resets & Defaults

  • Password Managers: Secure tools that generate and store unique, complex passwords for you.
  • Default Credentials: Factory-set usernames and passwords (like admin/admin) must be changed instantly.
  • Secure Resets: Reset compromised passwords via verified email/SMS links immediately upon suspicion.
