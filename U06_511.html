Value of Data and Information

Discover why data is considered the "new oil," understand the critical differences in data classification, and learn how to secure the digital assets that power our modern world.

Value of Data (Asset)

  • Data vs Information: Transforming raw facts into actionable insights.
  • Economic Value: Driving sales and customer retention through targeted marketing.
  • Operational Efficiency: Streamlining processes and predicting risks to save time and money.

Critical vs Non-Critical

  • Critical Data: Essential for operations, legal compliance, or financial stability.
  • Non-Critical Data: Supportive information; loss is inconvenient but not disastrous.
  • Consequences: Understanding the severe risk of critical data exposure.

PII (Personal Info)

  • Definition: Data that can directly or indirectly reveal a person's identity.
  • Direct PII: Unique identifiers like SSNs, Passports, and Driver's Licenses.
  • Indirect PII: Data like ZIP codes or birth dates that pose a risk when aggregated.

Management & Protection

  • Security Measures: Implementing robust protocols like encryption and strict access control.
  • Backup Strategies: Creating disaster recovery plans to survive data corruption.
  • Compliance: Adhering to legal standards and modifying approaches based on data tier.
