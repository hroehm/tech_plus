AAA Accounting & Auditing

Discover how computers track user actions to ensure accountability, prevent denial of actions (non-repudiation), and secure systems using logs, location data, and browser history.

AAA Triad & Accounting

  • Accountability: Ensuring users are held responsible for their actions on a system.
  • Non-Repudiation: Guaranteeing that users cannot deny performing an action.
  • Auditing: The process of reviewing records to find inconsistencies or security breaches.

System & Event Logs

  • Login/Auth Logs: Records of when users successfully or unsuccessfully attempt to sign in.
  • Access Logs: Detailed records of exactly what files, folders, or resources a user opened.
  • Network Logs: Documentation of traffic moving across the network infrastructure.

Location Tracking

  • IP Location: Identifying the geographical source of network traffic based on addressing.
  • Geofencing: Establishing virtual boundaries around real-world locations.
  • Impossible Travel: Detecting logins from physically distant locations in an impossibly short time.

Web Browser History

  • User Intent: Determining why a user took an action by reviewing their visited pages.
  • Session Management: Handling cookies and tracking tokens for prolonged access.
  • Privacy Controls: Managing how much data the browser is allowed to record or share.
