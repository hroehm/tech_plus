Device Interfaces: Display Standards & Technologies

Explore how display connections and display technologies transmit high-resolution video and audio signals across modern computing and multimedia environments.

Analog & Legacy (VGA & DVI)

  • VGA (Video Graphics Array): Classic analog video standard widely used for older monitors and projectors.
  • DVI (Digital Visual Interface): Hybrid interface supporting both digital and analog signals with superior image clarity.
  • Compatibility & Signals: DVI bridges the gap between older VGA displays and modern graphics cards.

High-Performance Digital (HDMI & DisplayPort)

  • HDMI: Transmits uncompressed digital video and multi-channel audio simultaneously with CEC support.
  • DisplayPort: Engineered for high-performance gaming monitors supporting high refresh rates and ultra-high resolutions.
  • Bandwidth & Quality: Modern standards powering 4K, 8K, and high frame-rate rendering.

Versatile Power & Data (USB-C)

  • Reversible Connector: Symmetric physical plug design eliminating orientation frustration.
  • All-in-One Cable: Capable of transferring data, display signals via Alt Mode, and power delivery simultaneously.
  • Modern Ecosystems: Standardizing ultrabooks, docking stations, and external 4K displays.

Wireless Display (Mirroring & Casting)

  • Mirroring: Exact real-time duplication of a source screen onto a target display device.
  • Casting: Streaming specific media content directly from apps while freeing the source device for other tasks.
  • Protocols: AirPlay, Miracast, and Google Chromecast standards enabling seamless wireless presentation.
