Device Interfaces: Display

Master the wired and wireless connections that carry video and audio signals to screens. Review this briefing before moving to the Lab Activity.

Legacy Video (VGA & DVI)

  • VGA: Older analog standard. Blue connector. Susceptible to interference. Does not carry audio.
  • DVI: Bridge technology. Carries both analog and digital signals. Better image quality than VGA. Does not carry audio.

Modern Digital (HDMI & DP)

  • HDMI: Standard for consumer electronics (TVs). Carries digital video and audio. Supports CEC.
  • DisplayPort (DP): Standard for high-performance PCs. Supports extreme refresh rates and resolutions. Carries audio and video.

The Universal Standard (USB-C)

  • Versatility: Reversible connector that handles video, audio, data, and power simultaneously.
  • Alt Mode: Uses "DisplayPort Alternate Mode" to transmit native video signals directly to modern monitors.
  • Adoption: Rapidly replacing legacy display ports on thin-and-light laptops.

Wireless Displays

  • Mirroring: Duplicates the exact screen of the source device onto the target display. (e.g., Miracast, AirPlay).
  • Casting: Sends specific content to the target display, freeing up the source device to do other tasks. (e.g., Chromecast, DLNA).
