Plain Text vs. Cipher Text

Discover the foundational concepts of encryption by understanding how readable information is mathematically scrambled to protect our privacy, secure our communications, and build digital trust.

Data States & Formats

  • Plain Text: Information stored in its original, readable format.
  • Cipher Text: Scrambled, unreadable information requiring a key.
  • Data at Rest vs Transit: Determines when text must be encrypted.

Encryption Algorithms

  • Encryption: The process of encoding plain text to prevent unauthorized access.
  • Algorithms: Step-by-step mathematical rules that govern the scrambling process.
  • Benefits: Protects privacy, secures info, builds trust, and prevents misuse.

Caesar Cipher & Keys

  • Julius Caesar: Earliest recorded use of shift ciphers for military messages.
  • The Key: The unique secret value that dictates how the algorithm shifts the data.
  • Decryption: Using the secret key to revert cipher text back to plain text.
