Availability Concerns

Availability ensures that systems and data are reliably accessible when authorized users need them. From hardware failures to DDoS attacks, explore the threats that cause downtime and the systems designed to prevent it.

Availability & The CIA Triad

  • Authorized Access: Ensuring only legitimate users can reach data when they need it.
  • Financial & Operational Loss: Downtime stops business, limits logistics, and costs massive revenue.
  • Reputation & Legal Risk: Outages erode user trust and can trigger compliance penalties (e.g., HIPAA).

Unexpected Issues

  • Hardware Failure: Components like hard drives and network switches fail due to age or heat.
  • Natural Disasters: Fires, floods, and hurricanes physically destroy data centers.
  • Power & Software: Grid blackouts and bad configuration code can instantly knock systems offline.

Controlled & Malicious Threats

  • Maintenance: Planned upgrades can cause extended, accidental outages if poorly tested.
  • DoS & DDoS: Overwhelming servers with fake traffic (e.g., Slowloris, Botnets).
  • Ransomware: Malware that encrypts systems entirely, holding availability hostage.

Fallback & Active Mitigation

  • Backups & Redundancy: Storing copies offsite and having duplicate N+1 hardware ready.
  • Failover & UPS: Automatic transition to battery power and backup generators during outages.
  • Load Balancing: Distributing network traffic across multiple servers to prevent overload.
