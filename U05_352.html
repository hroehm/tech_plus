Generative Artificial Intelligence

Explore how Generative AI works, what it can create, and how it is rapidly changing the way we write, code, and design.

What is Generative AI?

  • New Content Creation: Unlike older AI, it creates entirely new text, images, or code.
  • Neural Networks: Powered by complex machine learning models trained on vast datasets.
  • Examples: Tools like ChatGPT, DALL-E, and Microsoft Copilot.

The Art of the Prompt

  • Prompt: The specific instructions or questions given to the AI tool.
  • Clarity & Specificity: The quality of the output relies entirely on the clarity of the prompt.
  • Iterative Refinement: Improving results by adjusting and resubmitting prompts.

Use Cases: Text & Code

  • Speed & Support: Generates drafts and code structures in seconds.
  • Clueless Coding Buddy: It predicts code but doesn't actually understand the logic.
  • Creativity: A powerful tool for brainstorming and overcoming creative blocks.

Limitations & Risks

  • Hallucinations: AI can confidently make up false facts and information.
  • AI Drift & Bias: Models can degrade over time or adopt biases from bad training data.
  • Privacy: Users should never share personal or secure information in prompts.
