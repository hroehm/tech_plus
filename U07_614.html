Privacy in the Digital World

Privacy goes beyond basic security; it addresses exactly how personal information is collected, stored, used, and shared. Explore the vulnerabilities of digital communication and the regulations that protect users.

PII & Privacy Principles

  • PII: Personally Identifiable Information that reveals your identity (Name, Phone).
  • SPII: Sensitive PII that can cause serious harm if stolen (SSN, Medical Records).
  • Privacy Definition: The principle of maintaining control over how your data is used and stored.

Social Media & Email

  • Social Ownership: Platforms often own your uploaded content and share it with advertisers.
  • Encryption: End-to-End Encryption converts messages so only the recipient can read them.
  • Organizational Email: Avoid using school or work emails for private communications.

IM & File Sharing

  • Instant Messaging: Often lacks default encryption; users lose control over sent messages.
  • File Permissions: Giving too many permissions (e.g., Edit instead of View) poses severe risks.
  • Secure Practices: Utilize expiration dates and secure networks when sharing files.

AI & Regulations

  • AI Data: Generative AI often saves user inputs and chats to train future models.
  • GDPR & HIPAA: Strict government frameworks that mandate how data must be protected.
  • Cookies: Third-party cookies track users across multiple sites and compromise privacy.
